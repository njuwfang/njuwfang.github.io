## About Me

Since August 2026, I am an assistant professor at the College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence, United Arab Emirates University.

From July 2024 to August 2026, I was a postdoctoral research associate at the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh, hosted by [Chris Heunen](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/cheunen/) and [Robin Kaarsgaard](https://rkaarsgaard.github.io/).
I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2024, where I was fortunate to be supervised by Prof. [Mingsheng Ying](https://profiles.uts.edu.au/Mingsheng.Ying).
